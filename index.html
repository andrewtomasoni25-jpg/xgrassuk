The XGrass Difference

One Trade.
Mastered.

Most companies do a bit of everything. We chose grass — and we've spent over a decade getting obsessively good at it. While others treat turfing as one item on a long list of services, it's the only thing we do.

Every project gets the same treatment: stripped back to bare soil, levelled to precision, and finished with premium turf that roots deep and lasts. We don't subcontract, we don't cut corners, and we don't leave until the finish is right.

That single-minded focus is why our lawns still look immaculate months and years after installation — and why the majority of our work comes from referrals. When your neighbour's lawn looks like a bowling green, people ask questions.

Care
How It Works

From Bare Ground
to Brilliant Lawn

01

Site Assessment

We visit your property, assess the existing ground conditions, measure the area, and discuss exactly what you want from your new lawn.

02

Ground Preparation

Old turf removed, soil cultivated, levels corrected, drainage addressed. This is the work that separates a good lawn from a great one.

03

Turf Installation

Premium grade natural turf, precision-laid with tight seams and clean edges. Rolled, watered, and finished to a professional standard.

04

Aftercare

Detailed watering schedule, mowing guidance, and seasonal care advice. We make sure your new lawn establishes properly and stays healthy.
